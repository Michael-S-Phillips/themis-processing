To make nice-looking DCS radiance images that are not masked by TES coverage:
a=read_geo("I41931002.cub")
b=thm.rectify(a.data,ignore=-32768.0)
c=rrmnoise_pca(b.data)
d=dcs(cat(c[,,8],c[,,7],c[,,5],z),ignore=-32768.0,sample=cat(b.data[,,8],b.data[,,7],b.data[,,5],z))

Then project it:
b.data=d
z=thm.reconstitute(b)
a.data=z
write_geotiff(a,"I41931002_niceDCSrad875.tif")
